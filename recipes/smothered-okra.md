# Smothered Okra

*Cajun style*

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fresh okra, sliced into rounds
- 2 tbsp oil or bacon fat
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, or 2 fresh tomatoes, chopped
- Salt, black pepper, and cayenne to taste
- Optional: 1/2 lb shrimp or sliced andouille sausage

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Add the okra and cook it down, stirring often, for 15–20 minutes until the sliminess cooks off. This is the key step — don't rush it.
2. Add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic, and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the tomatoes and seasoning. If using sausage, add it now.
4. Lower the heat, cover, and smother gently for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally. If adding shrimp, toss them in for the last 5 minutes.
5. Serve over rice.
